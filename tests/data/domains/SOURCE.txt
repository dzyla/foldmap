cath_reference.json: domain choppings for calibrating foldmap's automatic domains (plan 12 Task 3, R2).

Source: CATH domain boundaries, through the PDBe API (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/api/mappings/cath/<pdb id>),
fetched 2026-10-15 for 26 classic multi-domain entries and the random-100 sample; one chain per entry (the one with
most domains); 33 chains with at least two CATH domains, plus 2LZM and 4AKE, which CATH keeps as one domain.
Residue numbers are the author numbers; a discontinuous domain is several [first, last] segments.

License: CATH data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Attribution: CATH (https://www.cathdb.info); Sillitoe I. et al. (2021) CATH: increased structural coverage of
functional space. Nucleic Acids Res 49:D266-D273. Accessed through PDBe (EMBL-EBI).
Changes: chains and domains selected as above, reshaped into this JSON.

The "literature" entries (2LZM, 4AKE) are hand choppings named per entry, not CATH data.

Each chain also carries its CATH superfamilies (from the same PDBe responses, fetched again 2026-10-16) and its
homology group: the chains sharing a superfamily with it, directly or through another. The groups with more than one
chain are 1LDM/1RM5/2HKI (Rossmann 3.40.50.720), the hexokinases 1BDG/1CZA/1HKB/2YHX with 4XEI (3.30.420.40),
1CSH/5CSC, 2YBE/3PGK, 1GPB/2C4M and 6FY9/6FYF.

cath_inputs.npz: what foldmap finds the automatic domains of each of the 33 chains from (domain_score.ChainInputs:
CA coordinates in thousandths of an Å, residue numbers, elements, their contacts, βα rows), so the ruled targets are
checked in CI (tests/test_domain_score.py). scripts/make_domain_fixture.py builds it from the structures and checks
that it gives the same domains as they do. The structures themselves are not committed (about 40 MB):
scripts/make_domain_cache.py puts them in $FOLDMAP_DOMAIN_CACHE (default ~/.cache/foldmap-domains), and the
end-to-end check on them skips without them.

How far the calibration generalises (plan 12 Task 3, fix round 1): the parameters were chosen on a seeded two thirds
of the 33 chains. The held-out third, drawn chain by chain, held near-duplicates of training chains (6FY9 of 6FYF,
2YBE of 3PGK, hexokinases 1CZA and 1HKB of 2YHX and 1BDG), so its numbers (overlap .81, recall .87) overstated it.
The seven held-out chains with no training homologue give overlap .82, recall .80, precision .70, F1 .72: recall
under the ruled .85. The held-out third is now drawn by homology group (domain_score.held_out); after the fact, it
gives overlap .80, recall .81, precision .68, F1 .72. All 33: overlap .85, recall .88, precision .76, F1 .79.
